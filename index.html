The story

A café where the dogs have seniority

Fresh bakes, hot cups and fur on every chair. We vacuum daily; the fur has tenure.

Bhaunk Café is a small corner between Phagwara and Jalandhar where good coffee, fresh bakes and friendly dogs share one roof. Everything on the menu is made in small batches each morning, and the dogs wander in and out as they please.

Come to read, to catch up, to work, or to do nothing at all next to something that wags. Nobody gets pushed into a cuddle. The dogs choose who gets lap privileges (usually everyone, immediately).

House rules are short: be gentle, let the dogs come to you, don’t feed them from your plate, and never stand between Moti and the AC vent. Bringing your own dog? Leash on, vaccinations up to date, introductions slow.
